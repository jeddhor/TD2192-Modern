# Handoff: M12-11 dynamic lighting, M12-12 bloom (IM-13, 2026-11-05)

**Hat:** Implementer. **Spec:** `docs/spec/enhanced/presentation.md` rev 2, §6 and §7. **ADR:** 0006.

## Done

- **`td_render::enhanced::lighting`**:
  - the half-resolution light map with the §6.2 falloff and the 4-px shadow march (cells holding p and the light are excluded), and the subtle/full presets;
  - the emissive set (`flight_emissive`);
  - modulation in linear light through exact sRGB tables (8-bit ↔ 14-bit; gain in 12-bit fixed point);
  - `LightStage`, the `PostStage` that runs the light map, then bloom.
- **`td_render::enhanced::bloom`**: the bright pass (Rec. 709 luminance > 0.9, or emissive), the 4×4 box mean, the separable 9-tap Gaussian (σ = 2), and the bilinear add in linear light.
- **`td_render::enhanced::lights`**: `flight_scene` builds the §6.1 sources and the occluder grid from `Flight::frame`, `Flight::drawn` and `World::solid`, with the margin. It uses `shot_look_in` and `mission_ship_pos_in`, so lights follow what is drawn in wide frames. Infra-red modes clamp to subtle.
- **`crates/td2192/src/flight/lights.rs`**:
  - `light_scene` and `emissive` for IM-12's `compose_enhanced`;
  - an ignored tuning test, `render_lit_frames`, that writes PNG files to `%TEMP%/td2192-lighting`. It needs `TD2192_DATA` and takes `TD2192_LIGHT_FLIGHTS` and `TD2192_LIGHT_TICKS`.
- **`crates/td2192/src/run.rs`** passes `LightStage` to `Enhanced::new`.
- The work is row-parallel with rayon, and every result is deterministic.
- **Tests:**
  - identity, radial symmetry, falloff, occluder darkening, emissive pixels, golden hashes for the light map and bloom;
  - light-list positions, and cannon visibility against `shot_look_in`;
  - the stage, and an ignored timing test, `crates/td-render/tests/enhanced_lighting_timing.rs`.
