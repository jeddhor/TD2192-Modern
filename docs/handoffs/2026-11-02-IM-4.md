# Handoff: IM-4, M6-07 in-flight HUD and mission-ship presentation (2026-11-02)

Hat: Implementer. Brief: [2026-11-02-IM-1c.md](2026-11-02-IM-1c.md). Branch off main `e9f65fe`.

## Done

- **`td_render::hud`** (new; `crates/td-render/src/hud.rs`), from `docs/spec/render/hud.md` rev 2:
  - `HudAssets` holds `td.win`, `td.wea`, `td.fac` (38 → 39 in frames 0..3), `td.lt2` and the exe table
    `hud.radar_tile_colours`.
  - `HudState` holds what `UI_DrawInstruments` reads at step 19. `HudState::from_flight(&Flight)` fills it from post-tick state.
  - `Hud` keeps the persistent bar (VGA rows 0..31), the ticker strip (VGA rows 735..742 with the
    352-pixel row wrap) and the palette entries:
    - `Hud::post(text, ink, TickerFont)` draws a message into the strip;
    - `Hud::step` runs one tick: guidance and lamps, the low-armour pulse, the armour and shield bars, the missile lamp and
      column, the radar, the weapon icon, the ticker blit and the F6 frame;
    - `Hud::draw` writes the dots into the playfield, the bar into rows 160..191 and the entries (`& 63`) into the palette.
  - The guidance line uses `td_sim::Real48` with the §3.5 constants. The unit test reproduces vector 4: the C-0002 dots.
- **Small text:** `small_text::draw_glyph` now takes any `Canvas` (a new trait in `framebuffer.rs`), so the ticker strip can be drawn into. Behaviour is unchanged.
- **Dev arena** (`crates/td2192/src/flight.rs`, `draw()` and its helpers only, plus the two fields that `new()` fills):
  - the HUD is stepped once per sim tick from `draw` (through a `RefCell`) and drawn after the playfield;
  - the flight's objective is posted at start (mission-logic §3.3);
  - mission ships are placed per mission-ships §4.7, with the target brackets, using the U-127 row correction.
    The placement and brackets are `td_render::flight::{mission_ship_pos, draw_mission_ship}` (new functions; the
    existing ones are unchanged).
  - Shots and explosions remain placeholders (U-128).
- **Docs:** U-127 and U-128 are in `docs/re/UNKNOWNS.md`; STATUS M6-07 is updated.

## Parity (`crates/td-render/tests/hud_parity.rs`, L5)

- **C-0002 f3543** (full memory dump; projectile arrays read through their far pointers): **0 differing pixels** in:
  - rows 160..191: bars, missile column, radar, weapon icon, ticker window and F6 lamp;
  - the animated DAC entries;
  - the whole playfield including the guidance dots.
- **C-0010, all 760 frames.** The rest of the bar, the weapon icon, the F6 lamp and the palette entries match every frame. Bars, missile column, radar and ticker window match the DGROUP dump of the same frame exactly, or a state one tick before or after it, or a half-drawn mix of the two.
  - Why the allowance: the HUD rows and the dump are not taken at the same instant of the frame, and the instruments are drawn straight into visible video memory. The ticker windows that differ from the dump show the next page-0 position (window = dumped ticker, not ticker − 1); f14710 shows the missile column between two overlapping `Fill4x2` blocks.
  - The test prints the per-element counts of exact frames.
  - Guidance dots are not asserted in C-0010: the playfield shows the page of the previous tick, and the dumps (every 35 frames) do not hold that state. C-0002 asserts them.
- **Mission-ship brackets (C-0009, `c0009_mission_ship_brackets_match`):** for every screen capture f3480..f4600, one of the dumps of the 6 frames before it puts all bracket pixels where the capture has them. A scratch check showed that without the 32-row correction (U-127) no dump does.
