## Test technique that worked (C-0009)

- **Sampling:** the state after tick t is the **last** dump frame whose DS:1718 equals t. The first frame of tick 1 is the state after setup.
- **Run the full `Flight::tick`, not parts of it.** Enemy fire and friendly fire matter: slot 1 is destroyed by a shot at tick 254.
- **Seed:** the seed after flight-1 setup equals 40 `Random` steps from 0. `Flight::with_mission(1, ..)` reproduces it exactly, so no seed patching is needed.
- **Muzzle table:** the test takes it from the dump at DS:035C (342 bytes).
- **Diagnostics:** the `diff` helper prints the slot arrays and the first differing record bytes.
- **IM-1's draw-page technique** (`tests/oracle_weapons.rs`) is the fallback if frame sampling gets fuzzy.

## Gotchas found in M8-04

- The AI's player tile (DS:9766/9767) is written only at the start of `AI_UpdateShips`. At setup it still holds the previous flight's value (0 at program start); the capture confirms it. That is why it is session state.
- Ship shields regenerate in the same tick after a hit (mission-ships pass 1). One existing test's expectation changed from 15 to 16 for this.
- A flight started with `Flight::start` begins on draw page 1 (mission-setup §3.1). `with_player` and `new` still start at 0; existing tests rely on that.
