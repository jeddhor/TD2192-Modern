Proposed for the next M8-05 pass: `pub hit_flash: u16` on `td_sim::Flight` (agreed with IM-3).
4. Mission-logic white-out (`mission.white_out`: DAC and flight palette all white). The arena does not apply it yet.
5. The HUD's `Pal_SetEntry` writes go into `g_FlightPalette` in the original. Here the IR formulas use plain `td.pal`, and the HUD overrides only its entries in the drawn palette (IM-4). Mirror `Hud::entries()` into P if a capture shows a difference.
