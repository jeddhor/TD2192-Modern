## Connecting the real flight (main session)

Implement `td_flow::FlightLauncher` in `crates/td2192` and pass it to `td_flow::Episode1::new`
instead of `PlaceholderLauncher`. `launch(ctx, n)` (n = 1..=21) returns a `FlightScreen`: a
`Screen` that pops itself when the flight ends and then reports `FlightResult { died,
aborted, lost, outcome, kills }`. The campaign stops the music and starts the flight song
before `launch`, and stops the music after the flight (music-cues §4.2), so the flight screen
should not touch music.
